## cit-001 · Sek-Ra-Tun (SEK-rah-TOON) — "the one who remembers the turnings"
cohort captive · elder woman · route-keeper · visual `elder` · leans Memory

**Voice:** unhurried, exact, ends arguments with a place-name. **Temperament:** patient until contradicted. **Skills:** the whole Circuit Song; weather by smell. **Pleasures:** being asked the way; river mussels; outliving people she disagreed with. **Shortcoming:** rations what she knows so she stays necessary.

**Background (immutable).** Sek-Ra-Tun held the Circuit Song, the walking song whose verses are the tribe's year: one verse to a stage, each naming a landmark, the water and whatever might kill you there. She learned it from an aunt who made her walk each verse before she was allowed to sing it, and she kept the rule. In her life she added eleven verses, which is a great many, and she was vain about every one. Two apprentices had most of the song. Nobody but her had all of it. She told herself that was caution. When Blackroot's trackers arrived at the autumn gathering by the tribe's own route, she understood that strangers had read the turnings from the ground, and that her secrecy had kept the song from no one except her own people. She came through the raid with her granddaughter Nim's hand in hers and did not let go of it afterwards. Her clearest good memory is small: the day Nim walked a whole stage without asking to be carried, and Sek-Ra-Tun pretended not to notice so the child wouldn't stop. In the cell she was near the back, because Nim's legs were short.

**Attitude to Duy:** wants his account before his suffering. Watches whether he learns names. **Wants in sanctuary:** to teach every verse to anyone who asks, and a route worth walking when nobody is fleeing.

**Relationships:** grandmother of cit-002 Nim; taught cit-004 Tav-Ri six verses and thinks him careless; forty years of argument with cit-016 Ume-Da-Ril; disputes a needle debt on cit-005 Orr-Ve-Kan's cord; mother-in-law of cit-020 Ase-Ro-Wen.

**First-meeting line:** "Sek-Ra-Tun. Route-keeper. Ask her the way to anywhere; she'll want to know why you're going."

**Conditional entries**
- `home:any` — "Chose the bed nearest the door. Old habit: a route-keeper sleeps where she can hear the weather."
- `cohabit-or-neighbor:cit-002` — "Nim is three doors away. Sek-Ra-Tun has counted the steps and says she hasn't."
- `celebration:first-song` — "Sang the first forty verses of the Circuit aloud, to everyone, for the first time in her life. Stopped there on purpose. 'Come back tomorrow.'"
- `reunion:cit-020` — "Ase-Ro-Wen came up the road. Sek-Ra-Tun, who always has a place-name ready, had nothing to say at all."

## cit-002 · Nim (NIM) — milk-name only
cohort captive · child, about eight · visual `child` · no bloc (children do not vote or work)

**Voice:** "And then what?" **Temperament:** curious, stubborn, bites when cornered. **Skills:** finds beetles anywhere; remembers songs after one hearing. **Pleasures:** beetle wing-cases sorted by colour; riding on a cook-basket. **Shortcoming:** hides food in her bedding. She started in captivity and has not stopped.

**Background (immutable).** Nim was born on the spring stage of the circuit and had walked about half of one on her own feet, which matters: a child earns a trail-name by walking a full circuit unaided, and Nim was counting. Her mother Ase-Ro-Wen was a climber who brought down nuts from the high canopy; her father Dak-Ro-Fen mended everything badly and cheerfully. At the autumn gathering they were among those who held the creek crossing so the children's group could get into the trees. Nim remembers being handed to her grandmother and told to hold on. She held on through the transport and in the dark cell, and she was holding on when the door opened and everyone ran. She does not understand that part. She remembers noise. Before all of it, her favourite place in the world was the top of Dof-Ma-Rek's basket, where the bread smell was, watching the path go backwards.

**Attitude to Duy:** has not connected the burned man in the street with the dark. Sudden bangs send her under the nearest table. She asks him questions nobody else will. **Wants in sanctuary:** her trail-name. There has to be a circuit to walk first.

**Relationships:** granddaughter of cit-001; daughter of cit-020 and cit-021; rides on cit-003's basket; shares beetles with cit-012 Kip; thinks cit-004 Tav-Ri is the fastest person alive.

**First-meeting line:** "Nim. One name so far. She is working on the second."

**Conditional entries**
- `arrival+cit-001 present` — "Found her grandmother in the refuge before anyone could show her where to look."
- `community-care:first-season` — "Stopped hiding bread in her blanket. Nobody mentioned it. Dof-Ma-Rek baked extra for a week anyway."
- `reunion:cit-020|cit-021` — "Held on. Was told, this time, that she could let go whenever she liked."
- `milestone:circuit-walked` (needs a walkable route; see handoff §4) — "Walked every stage on her own feet. The community gave her the second beat of her name. It is recorded here when it happens, not before."

## cit-003 · Dof-Ma-Rek (DOFF-mah-REK) — "the one who keeps it lit"
cohort captive · adult man · camp cook and coal-carrier · visual `cook` · leans Bowl

**Voice:** instructions shaped like questions ("You'll be sitting down, won't you?"). **Temperament:** generous, busy, immovable. **Skills:** feeding sixty from what forty brought; acorn flatbread; banking a coal to last three days. **Pleasures:** second helpings on someone else's plate. **Shortcoming:** cannot accept help, and feeds people rather than talk to them.

**Background (immutable).** The tribe never relit its fire. When a camp broke, a live coal went into a clay pot packed with ash and punk-wood, and the cook carried it to the next camp and woke it there. Dof-Ma-Rek was, by the count he was taught, the forty-first carrier of the same fire. He took the pot from his uncle at nineteen and had not let anyone else carry it since, including on the two occasions he had a fever and should have. In the raid a hobgoblin soldier kicked the pot off its sling while clearing the cook-ground. Dof-Ma-Rek went after the coal on his hands and knees and was netted there. He does not tell this story; others tell it, kindly, and he leaves the room. What he will talk about is Nim riding on his basket, and the year the hazelnuts failed and he fed the whole gathering on snails and nerve.

**Attitude to Duy:** will feed anyone who is hungry and resents that Duy qualifies. **Wants in sanctuary:** to light a fire he admits is a new one, and to hand the pot to someone while he is still well enough to watch them carry it.

**Relationships:** nephew and successor of the fortieth carrier (unauthored); Nim's basket; steady quarrel with cit-013 Mor-Ne-Dath over flour; owes cit-005 Orr-Ve-Kan for salt and has been told to forget it; cit-006 Yan-Su-Bel supplies his mushrooms and is the one person allowed in his cook-ground.

**First-meeting line:** "Dof-Ma-Rek, cook. He has already decided you're too thin."

**Conditional entries**
- `arrival` — "Asked where the kitchen was before he asked where he was."
- `job:farm-or-food` — "Back at work. Complained about the pots all day, singing."
- `celebration:shared-meal` — "Fed everyone. Sat down last. Was made, by general agreement, to eat a second helping himself."
- `milestone:new-fire` (proposed landmark; handoff §4) — "Lit a new fire and said aloud that it was new. Let Tav-Ri carry the pot as far as the gate."

## cit-004 · Tav-Ri (TAHV-ree) — two beats; hand-name not yet chosen
cohort captive · adolescent, about fifteen · apprentice lantern-bearer · visual `lantern` · leans Claw

**Voice:** fast, interrupts, swears by stages of the route. **Temperament:** competitive, loyal, furious. **Skills:** quickest climber of his year; night-walking with a shuttered lantern; six verses of the Circuit Song. **Pleasures:** being first; the hour before dawn. **Shortcoming:** acts, then thinks; cannot sit still with a feeling.

**Background (immutable).** Lantern-bearers walk ahead of a dusk move with a shuttered light, showing it only backwards so the column can follow and nothing in front can see. Tav-Ri was apprenticed to Hes-Vo-Lim and was good, and knew it. He earned his trail-name a year early and brought it up often. In the raid he got three children into the trees before a tracker's dog found them. In the dark cell he was nearest the door, because he is always nearest the way out, and he was the first to see it standing open with nobody in it. He screamed for everyone to run and ran. He did not see what was in the way until the first flash. He has decided that nineteen people died because he shouted. Nobody else has decided this. The memory he would rather keep is Hes-Vo-Lim handing him the lantern on the river stage and saying nothing, which from Hes-Vo-Lim was a speech.

**Attitude to Duy:** open fury. Counts Duy's failures and tells people the count. It is easier than counting his own. **Wants in sanctuary:** a hand-name that is not about the cell. He does not yet know what his work is.

**Relationships:** apprentice of cit-011 Hes-Vo-Lim; taught six verses by cit-001, who calls him careless; hero of cit-002 Nim; rival and closest friend of cit-014 Fen-Ya-Sool.

**First-meeting line:** "Tav-Ri. Two names. He got the second one early and will tell you so."

**Conditional entries**
- `arrival` — "Walked the edge of the refuge twice before he would sit. Found every way out. There was no need. He checked again."
- `reunion:cit-011` — "Hes-Vo-Lim looked him over and handed him a lantern. Neither said anything. It took a long time."
- `favor:claw-earned` — "Stopped telling people the count."

## cit-005 · Orr-Ve-Kan (OR-veh-KAHN) — "the one who walks the edge"
cohort captive · middle-aged man · edge-trader · visual `merchant` · leans Hand

**Voice:** warm, quick, everything is an offer. **Temperament:** sociable, shrewd, privately frightened. **Skills:** four trade tongues, badly but usefully; knot-cord accounts; judging a stranger's purse by their shoes. **Pleasures:** a good loss-leader; gossip; salt. **Shortcoming:** bargains when he should apologise.

**Background (immutable).** Most of the tribe never left the trees. Orr-Ve-Kan did, four times a year, carrying dried mushrooms, resin and cordage out to the forest-edge markets and coming back with needles, salt and news. He kept the tribe's debts on a knotted cord and officially forgave none of them; in practice he forgot to collect from anyone having a hard season and would deny it if asked. He loved the markets. He loved being the one who knew how outsiders thought. After the raid he went back over every trip, every stranger who had asked a friendly question about where his people wintered, and he cannot find the mistake and cannot stop looking. No one has told him he was followed. No one has told him he wasn't. The memory he keeps for himself is his nephew Lud-Ro-Ken's first trade, in which the boy paid double for a bad knife and was so proud that Orr-Ve-Kan let him keep thinking it was a bargain for a full year.

**Attitude to Duy:** recognises a debtor and is the most practical voice about him: a man working is worth more to the dead than a man burning. **Wants in sanctuary:** a market, and one day Ratkin from elsewhere walking into it with goods he has never seen.

**Relationships:** uncle and master of cit-018 Lud-Ro-Ken; holds a cord-debt against nearly everyone, including cit-003 (salt) and cit-001 (needles, disputed); avoided by cit-015 Ras-Ti-Vok, who has said aloud what Orr-Ve-Kan fears.

**First-meeting line:** "Orr-Ve-Kan, trader. You owe him something already. He'll let you know what."

**Conditional entries**
- `job:yard-or-storehouse` — "Counted the storehouse twice and pronounced it 'a start'."
- `home:apartment` — "Chose neighbours on both sides. He says it is good for business. There is no business yet."
- `reunion:cit-018` — "Told Lud-Ro-Ken the truth about the knife. Lud-Ro-Ken had known for years."
- `milestone:otherworld-arrival` (later scope) — reserved.

**FLAG:** his fear touches the open Blackroot / goblin-trading-post question. It is written as unverified on purpose. Do not confirm or deny in any text.

## cit-006 · Yan-Su-Bel (YAHN-soo-BELL) — "the one who plants for next year"
cohort captive · adult woman · log-seeder · visual `farmer` · leans Bowl, undecided

**Voice:** quiet; the joke lands a minute after she has left. **Temperament:** patient, private, conflict-averse. **Skills:** mushroom spawn, reading rot, knowing which fallen tree will feed people in a year. **Pleasures:** finding her own old work still fruiting; rain on bark. **Shortcoming:** plans for next year and neglects today; leaves rather than argue.

**Background (immutable).** A people who move cannot keep fields, so the tribe farmed forward. Along every stage, Yan-Su-Bel and the seeders before her bored fallen logs and plugged the holes with spawn, so that the tribe passing a year later would find food waiting that nobody had carried. It is work for someone comfortable not seeing results. She was. She marked each log with a small cut pattern of her own, and her best day was finding a log she had seeded as an apprentice, twelve years on, still fruiting, her mark grown nearly shut. She shared a tent for nine years with Pel-Ta-Shu, the spring-finder, who is as loud as Yan-Su-Bel is quiet; neither has ever explained the arrangement and nobody has needed them to. During the raid she did the thing she always does and slipped away from the fighting, and she was taken at the tree-line going back for Pel-Ta-Shu. She has not decided how she feels about either half of that.

**Attitude to Duy:** reserves judgment for a season. She will look at whether what he builds is still standing, and still feeding people, next year. **Wants in sanctuary:** to eat something in the same year she planted it. Whether that means staying put, she has not said.

**Relationships:** tent-mate of cit-008 Pel-Ta-Shu; supplies cit-003 and is the only one allowed in his cook-ground; trades spawn for seed with cit-007 Ghe-Lo-Mar.

**First-meeting line:** "Yan-Su-Bel. She plants things and walks away from them. It works out."

**Conditional entries**
- `job:farm` — "Marked the first farm bed with her old cut-pattern, small, where you'd have to look for it."
- `production:first-food` — "Ate a mushroom from a bed she planted herself, this season. Reported that it was 'strange, not waiting'."
- `cohabit-or-neighbor:cit-008` — "Pel-Ta-Shu moved in singing. Yan-Su-Bel says she'd forgotten how loud it was. She has not asked her to stop."
- `home:burrow` — "A door she can close. She leaves it open."
