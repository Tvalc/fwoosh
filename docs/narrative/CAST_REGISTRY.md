## cit-001 · Sek-Ra-Tun (SEK-rah-TOON) — "the one who remembers the road"
cohort captive · elder woman · road-keeper · visual `elder` · leans Memory

**Voice:** unhurried, exact, ends arguments with a place-name. **Temperament:** patient until contradicted. **Skills:** the whole Road Song; weather by smell. **Pleasures:** being asked the way; river mussels; outliving people she disagreed with. **Shortcoming:** rations what she knows so she stays necessary.

**Background (immutable).** Sek-Ra-Tun held the Road Song. Each verse is a village her people built and were driven out of, and the road they took to the next; sung backwards it is the way home to a kingdom nobody living has seen. The oldest verses are in words so worn that no one knows what they mean, and the first verse, which ought to hold the kingdom's name, is only a sound. In her life she added two verses. Nobody wants to add one. Two apprentices had most of the song; nobody but her had all of it, because a people in hiding do not leave their road lying about. When Blackroot's trackers reached Hollowbeech by following the last move, she understood that strangers had read the road off the ground, and that her secrecy had kept the song from no one except her own people. She came through the raid with her granddaughter Nim's hand in hers and did not let go of it afterwards. Her clearest good memory is small: the day Nim walked to the far fields and back without asking to be carried, and Sek-Ra-Tun pretended not to notice so the child wouldn't stop. In the cell she was near the back, because Nim's legs were short.

**Attitude to Duy:** wants his account before his suffering. Watches whether he learns names. **Wants in sanctuary:** to teach every verse to anyone who asks, and to add a verse, for once, about a place nobody made them leave.

**Relationships:** grandmother of cit-002 Nim; taught cit-004 Tav-Ri six verses and thinks him careless; forty years of argument with cit-016 Ume-Da-Ril; disputes a needle debt on cit-005 Orr-Ve-Kan's cord; mother of cit-021 Dak-Ro-Fen and mother-in-law of cit-020 Ase-Ro-Wen; called cit-022 Hal-Ne-Dur 'uncle' in her youth.

**First-meeting line:** "Sek-Ra-Tun. Road-keeper. Ask her the way to anywhere; she'll want to know why you're going."

**Conditional entries**

- `cohabit-or-neighbor:cit-002` — "Nim is three doors away. Sek-Ra-Tun has counted the steps and says she hasn't."

## cit-002 · Nim (NIM) — milk-name only
cohort captive · child, about eight · visual `child` · no bloc (children do not vote or work)

**Voice:** "And then what?" **Temperament:** curious, stubborn, bites when cornered. **Skills:** finds beetles anywhere; remembers songs after one hearing. **Pleasures:** beetle wing-cases sorted by colour; riding on a cook-basket. **Shortcoming:** hides food in her bedding. She started in captivity and has not stopped.

**Background (immutable).** Nim was born in Hollowbeech's first spring and never lived anywhere else, which among her people is a kind of luck and a kind of problem. A child earns a trail-name by walking the road between villages on their own feet, and Hollowbeech had stood nine years. Nim wanted her second name badly and had not yet worked out what would have to happen for her to get it. The adults had, and did not explain. Her mother Ase-Ro-Wen was a climber who brought down nuts from the high canopy; her father Dak-Ro-Fen mended everything badly and cheerfully. On the night Blackroot came they were among those who held the creek crossing so the children's group could get into the trees. Nim remembers being handed to her grandmother and told to hold on. She held on through the transport and in the dark cell, and she was holding on when the door opened and everyone ran. She does not understand that part. She remembers noise. Before all of it, her favourite place in the world was the top of Dof-Ma-Rek's basket, where the bread smell was, going round the lanes on oven days.

**Attitude to Duy:** has not connected the burned man in the street with the dark. Sudden bangs send her under the nearest table. She asks him questions nobody else will. **Wants in sanctuary:** her trail-name. Nobody is ever going to be driven out of sanctuary, so somebody will have to invent a new way to earn one.

**Relationships:** granddaughter of cit-001; daughter of cit-020 and cit-021; rides on cit-003's basket; shares beetles with cit-012 Kip; thinks cit-004 Tav-Ri is the fastest person alive.

**First-meeting line:** "Nim. One name so far. She is working on the second."

**Conditional entries**
- `arrival+cit-001 present` — "Found her grandmother in the refuge before anyone could show her where to look."
- `community-care:first-season` — "Stopped hiding bread in her blanket. Nobody mentioned it. Dof-Ma-Rek baked extra for a week anyway."
- `reunion:cit-020|cit-021` — "Held on. Was told, this time, that she could let go whenever she liked."
- `milestone:trail-name` (no system; see handoff §4) — "Walked a road she chose, all of it, on her own feet. The first trail-name ever given for a journey nobody was forced to make. It is recorded here when it happens, not before."

## cit-003 · Dof-Ma-Rek (DOFF-mah-REK) — "the one who keeps it lit"
cohort captive · adult man · village cook and coal-carrier · visual `cook` · leans Bowl

**Voice:** instructions shaped like questions ("You'll be sitting down, won't you?"). **Temperament:** generous, busy, immovable. **Skills:** feeding sixty from what forty brought; acorn flatbread; banking a coal to last three days. **Pleasures:** second helpings on someone else's plate. **Shortcoming:** cannot accept help, and feeds people rather than talk to them.

**Background (immutable).** Her people never relit their fire. It burned in the common oven of whatever village they had, and when a village was lost a live coal went into a clay pot packed with ash and punk-wood, and the cook carried it down the road and woke it in the next one. Dof-Ma-Rek was, by the count he was taught, the forty-first carrier of the same fire. He took the pot from his uncle at nineteen and had not let anyone else carry it since, including on the two occasions he had a fever and should have. In the raid a hobgoblin soldier kicked the pot off its sling while clearing the oven-yard. Dof-Ma-Rek went after the coal on his hands and knees and was netted there. He does not tell this story; others tell it, kindly, and he leaves the room. What he will talk about is Nim riding on his basket, and the year the hazelnuts failed and he fed the whole village on snails and nerve.

**Attitude to Duy:** will feed anyone who is hungry and resents that Duy qualifies. **Wants in sanctuary:** to light a fire he admits is a new one, and to hand the pot to someone while he is still well enough to watch them carry it.

**Relationships:** nephew and successor of cit-022 Hal-Ne-Dur, the fortieth carrier; pot-sling mended many times by cit-021 Dak-Ro-Fen; Nim's basket; steady quarrel with cit-013 Mor-Ne-Dath over flour; owes cit-005 Orr-Ve-Kan for salt and has been told to forget it; cit-006 Yan-Su-Bel supplies his mushrooms and is the one person allowed in his oven-yard.

**First-meeting line:** "Dof-Ma-Rek, cook. He has already decided you're too thin."

**Conditional entries**
- `arrival` — "Asked where the kitchen was before he asked where he was."
- `job:farm-or-food` — "Back at work. Complained about the pots all day, singing."
- `celebration:shared-meal` — "Fed everyone. Sat down last. Was made, by general agreement, to eat a second helping himself."
- `milestone:new-fire` (proposed landmark; handoff §4) — "Lit a new fire and said aloud that it was new. Let Tav-Ri carry the pot as far as the gate."

## cit-004 · Tav-Ri (TAHV-ree) — two beats; hand-name not yet chosen
cohort captive · adolescent, about fifteen · apprentice lantern-bearer · visual `lantern` · leans Claw

**Voice:** fast, interrupts, swears by the names of lost villages. **Temperament:** competitive, loyal, furious. **Skills:** quickest climber of his year; night-walking with a shuttered lantern; six verses of the Road Song. **Pleasures:** being first; the hour before dawn. **Shortcoming:** acts, then thinks; cannot sit still with a feeling.

**Background (immutable).** Lantern-bearers keep the night watch round the lanes, and when a village is lost they walk ahead of the column with a shuttered light, showing it only backwards so their people can follow and nothing in front can see. Tav-Ri was apprenticed to Hes-Vo-Lim and was good, and knew it. He walked the whole road to Hollowbeech at six with his own pack, got his trail-name years before his friends, and brought it up often. In the raid he got three children into the trees before a tracker's dog found them. In the dark cell he was nearest the door, because he is always nearest the way out, and he was the first to see it standing open with nobody in it. He screamed for everyone to run and ran. He did not see what was in the way until the first flash. He has decided that nineteen people died because he shouted. Nobody else has decided this. The memory he would rather keep is Hes-Vo-Lim handing him the lantern for his first night watch and saying nothing, which from Hes-Vo-Lim was a speech.

**Attitude to Duy:** open fury. Counts Duy's failures and tells people the count. It is easier than counting his own. **Wants in sanctuary:** a hand-name that is not about the cell. He does not yet know what his work is.

**Relationships:** apprentice of cit-011 Hes-Vo-Lim; taught six verses by cit-001, who calls him careless; hero of cit-002 Nim; rival and closest friend of cit-014 Fen-Ya-Sool.

**First-meeting line:** "Tav-Ri. Two names. He got the second one early and will tell you so."

## cit-005 · Orr-Ve-Kan (OR-veh-KAHN) — "the one who walks the edge"
cohort captive · middle-aged man · edge-trader · visual `merchant` · leans Hand

**Voice:** warm, quick, everything is an offer. **Temperament:** sociable, shrewd, privately frightened. **Skills:** four trade tongues, badly but usefully; knot-cord accounts; judging a stranger's purse by their shoes. **Pleasures:** a good loss-leader; gossip; salt. **Shortcoming:** bargains when he should apologise.

**Background (immutable).** A people in hiding do not go to town. Orr-Ve-Kan did, four times a year, carrying dried mushrooms, resin and cordage out to the market towns and coming back with needles, salt and news. He kept the village's debts on a knotted cord and officially forgave none of them; in practice he forgot to collect from anyone having a hard season and would deny it if asked. He loved the markets. He loved being the one who knew how outsiders thought. After the raid he went back over every trip, every stranger who had asked a friendly question about where his people had settled this time, and he cannot find the mistake and cannot stop looking. No one has told him he was followed. No one has told him he wasn't. The memory he keeps for himself is his nephew Lud-Ro-Ken's first trade, in which the boy paid double for a bad knife and was so proud that Orr-Ve-Kan let him keep thinking it was a bargain for a full year.

**Attitude to Duy:** recognises a debtor and is the most practical voice about him: a man working is worth more to the dead than a man burning. **Wants in sanctuary:** a market, and one day Ratkin from elsewhere walking into it with goods he has never seen.

**Relationships:** uncle and master of cit-018 Lud-Ro-Ken; holds a cord-debt against nearly everyone, including cit-003 (salt) and cit-001 (needles, disputed); avoided by cit-015 Ras-Ti-Vok, who has said aloud what Orr-Ve-Kan fears.

**First-meeting line:** "Orr-Ve-Kan, trader. You owe him something already. He'll let you know what."

## cit-006 · Yan-Su-Bel (YAHN-soo-BELL) — "the one who plants for next year"
cohort captive · adult woman · log-seeder · visual `farmer` · leans Bowl, undecided

**Voice:** quiet; the joke lands a minute after she has left. **Temperament:** patient, private, conflict-averse. **Skills:** mushroom spawn, reading rot, knowing which fallen tree will feed people in a year. **Pleasures:** finding her own old work still fruiting; rain on bark. **Shortcoming:** plans for next year and neglects today; leaves rather than argue.

**Background (immutable).** A people who expect to be driven out learn to farm where no one looks. Yan-Su-Bel bored fallen logs in the woods around each village and plugged the holes with spawn, which feeds people quietly and cannot be burned like a field. On every move she did something the seeders before her had taught her: she seeded logs along the road behind them, for whoever was driven down it next. It is work for someone comfortable not seeing results. She was. She marked each log with a small cut pattern of her own, and her best day was on the road to Hollowbeech, passing woods she had worked as an apprentice twelve years before, and finding a log still fruiting with her mark grown nearly shut. She shared a house for nine years with Pel-Ta-Shu, the spring-finder, who is as loud as Yan-Su-Bel is quiet; neither has ever explained the arrangement and nobody has needed them to. During the raid she did the thing she always does and slipped away from the fighting, and she was taken at the tree-line going back for Pel-Ta-Shu. She has not decided how she feels about either half of that.

**Attitude to Duy:** reserves judgment for a season. She will look at whether what he builds is still standing, and still feeding people, next year. **Wants in sanctuary:** to eat something in the same year she planted it. Whether that means staying put, she has not said.

**Relationships:** housemate of cit-008 Pel-Ta-Shu; supplies cit-003 and is the only one allowed in his oven-yard; trades spawn for seed with cit-007 Ghe-Lo-Mar.

**First-meeting line:** "Yan-Su-Bel. She plants things and walks away from them. It works out."

**Conditional entries**
- `job:farm` — "Marked the first farm bed with her old cut-pattern, small, where you'd have to look for it."
- `production:first-food` — "Ate a mushroom from a bed she planted herself, this season. Reported that it was 'strange, not waiting'."

## cit-008 · Pel-Ta-Shu (PELL-tah-SHOO) — "the one who sings the water up"
cohort captive · adult woman · spring-finder · visual `wellkeeper` · leans Hearth

**Voice:** loud, musical, narrates her own day. **Temperament:** open-handed, quick to cry and quick to finish crying. **Skills:** finds water by the sound her voice makes against a slope; digs a seep in an hour; knows every lullaby her people have. **Pleasures:** harmony, other people's babies, the first cup from a new spring. **Shortcoming:** fills every silence, including the ones people need.

**Background (immutable).** A village goes where the spring-finder says. On the last move Pel-Ta-Shu walked ahead of the column singing at hillsides and listening to what came back, and where she stopped and pointed, people put their packs down. That was Hollowbeech, and every well in it was hers. She was almost never wrong and she enjoyed it enormously. Her sister Eth-Wa-Min delivered the village's children; Pel-Ta-Shu sang to them afterwards, so that most of the village under thirty had heard her voice before they had opened their eyes. For nine years she shared a house with Yan-Su-Bel, who barely speaks. People assumed Pel-Ta-Shu did the talking for both. In fact she talks less in that house than anywhere, and it is the only place she does. In the raid she stood in the open and sang the scatter-call, the one that means every family for itself into the woods, until she was knocked down. Some got away because of it. None of them stayed free. She knows both of those things.

**Attitude to Duy:** cried for him the first time she saw what the fire did to his arms, was angry at herself for it, and has not settled which reaction was right. **Wants in sanctuary:** a spring that stays found, and a house loud with other people.

**Relationships:** housemate of cit-006 Yan-Su-Bel; sister of cit-019 Eth-Wa-Min; sang at the births of cit-002 Nim, cit-004 Tav-Ri and cit-012 Kip.

**First-meeting line:** "Pel-Ta-Shu finds water by singing at it. You will hear her before you see her."

**Conditional entries**
- `arrival` — "Sang the whole way up. The refuge heard her coming and had a cup waiting."
- `cohabit-or-neighbor:cit-006` — "Moved in singing. Went quiet around the second evening. Yan-Su-Bel says that is how she knows Pel-Ta-Shu is home."
- `celebration:music` — "Started a round. By the third time through, forty people had a part."

## cit-020 · Ase-Ro-Wen (AH-seh-roh-WEN) — "the one who goes up"
cohort village (died in the raid) · adult woman · canopy climber · visual `ratkin` · leans Claw

**Voice:** brief, amused, talks with her hands full. **Temperament:** fearless about heights, cautious about people. **Skills:** free-climbing; rope-setting; dropping a nut into a basket from forty feet. **Pleasures:** wind at the top of a tree; her daughter's questions; beating Dak-Ro-Fen down a trunk. **Shortcoming:** assumes anything can be solved by going and doing it herself.

**Background (immutable).** Ase-Ro-Wen harvested the high canopy, where the best nuts are and where a fall kills. She married into Sek-Ra-Tun's family and the two of them respected each other the way two people do who each think the other is too stubborn. Her daughter Nim was born in Hollowbeech's first spring, and Ase-Ro-Wen carried her up a beech at six weeks old to show her the top of the forest, which Sek-Ra-Tun has never forgiven and Nim cannot remember. When Blackroot closed the ring around Hollowbeech, she and Dak-Ro-Fen went with the others to the creek crossing, the one gap the soldiers had not yet shut, and held it while the children's group went through into the trees. She put Nim into Sek-Ra-Tun's arms herself. She died at the crossing believing the children had got clear. They had not. She burned until she was carried out, and she learned what happened to Nim only on arriving.

**Attitude to Duy:** he took the fire off her, and he shot her child. She holds both without trying to make them balance. She is the hardest vote in the Claw and the one most likely to say so to his face. **Wants in sanctuary:** to take Nim up a tree again, this time with permission.

**Relationships:** mother of cit-002 Nim; partner of cit-021 Dak-Ro-Fen; daughter-in-law of cit-001 Sek-Ra-Tun; taught cit-014 Fen-Ya-Sool to climb.

## cit-021 · Dak-Ro-Fen (DAHK-roh-FEN) — "the one who makes it do"
cohort village (died in the raid) · adult man · mender · visual `mason` · leans Hand

**Voice:** cheerful, apologetic, always mid-repair. **Temperament:** easy, optimistic past the evidence. **Skills:** mends anything well enough to last the season; splices rope; whistles. **Pleasures:** a fix that holds; making Nim laugh; losing races to Ase-Ro-Wen. **Shortcoming:** never makes anything new or properly, because nothing his people built had ever been allowed to stand.

**Background (immutable).** A people driven out four times in living memory do not own anything new. Dak-Ro-Fen went about with a bag of cord, pitch and odd bits of wood and fixed what failed: cart-frames, sandals, door-hinges, once a cracked drum with a snail shell and resin. Nothing he mended was pretty and nearly all of it held. He and Ase-Ro-Wen shared the second beat of their names by coincidence, both having walked the same road as children, in the wet year the village before Hollowbeech was lost, and he considered this the luckiest thing that ever happened to him. He was the parent who answered "and then what?" every time, for as long as Nim could keep asking. At the creek crossing he had no weapon and used a fence-post. He died there a little before Ase-Ro-Wen did. He burned until he was carried out.

**Attitude to Duy:** inclined to forgive early, and knows it, and is trying to wait until he has seen more. Watches how Duy builds: whether it is made to last or made to do. **Wants in sanctuary:** to build one thing properly, from new wood, and live in it long enough to see it not break.

**Relationships:** father of cit-002 Nim; partner of cit-020 Ase-Ro-Wen; son of cit-001 Sek-Ra-Tun; mended cit-003 Dof-Ma-Rek's pot-sling more times than either admits.

**First-meeting line:** "Dak-Ro-Fen fixes things. He has noticed three things wrong with the refuge and is very happy about it."

**Conditional entries**
- `reunion:cit-002` — "Nim asked what happened next. He said he didn't know yet, and that it was the best answer he'd ever had."
- `job:yard-or-storehouse` — "Handed new timber for the first time in his life. Held it a while before cutting."

- `reunion:cit-003` — "Looked at the empty pot-sling and offered to mend it. Dof-Ma-Rek said it wasn't broken. Dak-Ro-Fen mended it anyway."

## cit-022 · Hal-Ne-Dur (HAHL-neh-DOOR) — "the one who carried it before"
cohort village (ancestor; died of age at Hollowbeech, before the raid) · elder man · fortieth coal-carrier · visual `elder` · leans Hearth

**Voice:** slow, amused, gives advice as weather reports. **Temperament:** settled; difficult to alarm. **Skills:** fire in any rain; knowing when to stop talking. **Pleasures:** a nap beside something cooking; being proved right gently. **Shortcoming:** let his nephew take everything on alone, because it was easier than arguing.

**Background (immutable).** Hal-Ne-Dur kept his people's fire for thirty-one years and carried it down the road twice. He handed the pot to his nephew Dof-Ma-Rek when his knees gave out, spent his last years on the bench by the common oven criticising the cooking, and died in his sleep in Hollowbeech's second winter, warm, with the fire in sight. He had a good death by any measure his people had. Then he burned. He had never heard of Blackroot, never saw a hobgoblin, and knew nothing of the raid or the cell until the refuge told him. He arrived to find the fire he had kept alive his whole life had gone out in his nephew's hands, and his nephew unable to look at him.

**Attitude to Duy:** Duy never harmed him. Duy carried him out of a fire he had been in longer than he can reckon. He says so, plainly, in front of people who find it hard to hear. **Wants in sanctuary:** to see Dof-Ma-Rek light a new fire and stop apologising for the old one.

**Relationships:** uncle and predecessor of cit-003 Dof-Ma-Rek; old friend of cit-001 Sek-Ra-Tun, who was young when he was middle-aged; never met cit-002 Nim.

**First-meeting line:** "Hal-Ne-Dur kept the fire before Dof-Ma-Rek did. He died in his bed years ago. He would like to know what he missed."
