# First Ratkin Judgment

Build `2026-09-15-judgment-1` turns restoration into a visible five-term goal. Khet-Tak-Tor summons the first judgment only when every term is true:

1. All five districts have actually been cleared.
2. At least nineteen Ratkin have ascended through rescues.
3. Two Ratkin Burrows are sealed and connected to the gate road.
4. A connected sealed Mushroom Farm has produced at least one food.
5. A connected sealed Salvage Yard and Storehouse exist, and the Yard has produced at least one material.

The two Burrows supply enough workers for the Farm and Yard to operate together. Requiring produced food and material proves the supply chain has run; merely holding the four bootstrap food or loading an old material balance does not satisfy production.

The town displays all five terms from the start. When the final term completes, the next return to Ashford or exit from the Quarter opens a three-line present-tense judgment. Khet-Tak-Tor acknowledges that Duy rebuilt enough for Ratkin society to survive, then states that release still requires Ratkin favor. This hearing does not release or resurrect Duy.

The final authority belongs collectively to the rebuilt Ratkin community. Five social blocs judge Duy after the restoration hearing:

1. **The Hearth** judges shelter. Seal one additional connected Burrow after the hearing.
2. **The Bowl** judges sustenance. Produce eight new food after the hearing.
3. **The Hand** judges rebuilding. Produce six new materials after the hearing.
4. **The Claw** judges protection. Ascend twelve more Ratkin and win one more Arbiter trial after the hearing.
5. **The Memory** judges truth. Read all fifteen optional diary chapters.

Each completed proof permanently earns its bloc's support. Four votes release and resurrect Duy. The optional Memory path lets the deeper story remain optional for release; any four blocs are sufficient. A unanimous five-vote verdict awards the strongest Chit-tat-to's Invoice reward tier plus eligibility for the missable Ratkin recruit. Khet-Tak-Tor administers the covenant, presents the evidence, announces their verdict and executes the resulting sentence; he cannot personally forgive Duy's debt or replace the community's judgment with his own.

The final verdict is a five-line present-tense scene. Khet-Tak-Tor reports the count, confirms that the community has paid Duy's sentence, ends the fire's authority over him and sends him back toward Cuong and Diep. The Shrine keeps all five blocs and their progress visible, explains the four-vote release threshold, and continues tracking a possible fifth vote after release.

Larger settlements, shorter roads, extra storage and optimized priorities remain optional. They may later affect Invoice rewards, cosmetics or postgame progression; copy quotas do not gate this first judgment.

The save stays at meta version 1. `city.producedFood` and `city.producedMaterials` are nonnegative lifetime production proofs. The first hearing captures baselines for saves, food, materials, connected Burrows and Arbiter wins so existing accomplishments do not instantly satisfy the new favor phase. Earned votes, verdict readiness, release and unanimity persist. An older save that already heard the first judgment receives its baselines on the next town entry and keeps all prior progress.
