## Locked story direction

- Monsters wiped out the nomadic forest tribe's village and captured its last nineteen survivors. Duy and Cuong later killed those unarmed captives. Keep the village destruction and later cell killings distinct. That whole society is the first rebuilt in Fwoosh sanctuary. Rebuilding includes growth, expansion and a better future, not merely restoring a former state.
- Every resident must have an individually authored name, background and lore. Build an expanding authored backlog beyond nineteen; nineteen is only an initial writing batch, not a cast/population limit or an approved exact arrival order. No procedural/recombined biography fallback. Include the wider tribe and its history, with eventual other-world arrivals later. Do not add victims to Duy and Cuong's nineteen cell killings or invent additional living survivors of the tribe's destruction.
- Sanctuary is the Ratkin multiversal afterlife, distinct from Ledger's living material-world settlement. Residents retain identity and receive eternal life/protection within sanctuary. Children can mature; adults cease aging. Voluntary departure means mortality while away and return restores protection. Initial departures are lore, not an implemented expedition system.
- The refuge is already joyful. Singles, friendships, shared households, couples and families are equally valid. Do not force romance, parenthood or a single attitude toward Duy. Residents have aspirations and lives beyond their victimhood or usefulness to the player.
- Khet-Tak-Tor is the Ratkin Arbiter. Keith is not a character. The Arbiter administers the collective verdict and cannot personally forgive Duy. Four of five blocs release Duy; unanimity improves the future Invoice/recruit outcome. Preserve the shared near-death covenant as recorded in CANON.md.
- Flaming rescue draws painful heat into Duy; pain grows with carried heat. A calm/cinder rescue spends one carried heat, and demon kills supply heat. All three rescue states ascend the Ratkin. Do not write that Duy must let someone burn to rescue them.
- Duy's opening confusion concerns the larger mystery. Readers must understand immediate action, physical cause/effect and stakes. The world keeps moving while he struggles to understand.

Aim for the craft qualities Tony values in Brandon Sanderson: lucid action and cause/effect, comprehensible magic with meaningful costs, purposeful worldbuilding, fair clues, earned reversals and emotional payoff. Write an original Fwoosh voice; do not imitate an author's signature prose or promise equivalent literary quality.
