No procedural/recombined biography fallback. Include the wider displaced people and their history, with eventual other-world arrivals later. - Duy's opening confusion concerns the larger mystery. Readers must understand immediate action, physical cause/effect and stakes. The world keeps moving while he struggles to understand.

The villagers resisted; Blackroot destroyed Hollowbeech and captured its last nineteen survivors, whom Duy and Cuong later killed in the cell.
